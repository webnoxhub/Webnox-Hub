Start with the outcome you want

If the business needs more relevant quote requests, begin with a site that explains what you offer, who you help and how a customer can get in touch. If customers already know what they need and can purchase directly, start by mapping the product catalog, checkout and delivery experience. This gives the website a job you can test before you invest in more traffic.

Once the path works, choose where to improve it. Search visibility can help customers find the right service page. Paid campaigns can send a specific audience to a relevant offer. A product or automation project makes sense when the work involves a repeatable task that needs its own workflow.
