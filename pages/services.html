How the services fit together

A service business can begin with the page that explains its offer and a working inquiry flow, then use SEO or paid ads when it knows what a good lead looks like. An online store usually needs a different starting point: useful collections, product details, shipping information and a purchase flow the team can test from a phone.

If your team already has customers but spends time passing data between tools, bring one repeatable process to the discussion. A targeted integration may be enough. If customers need to sign in and complete the task themselves, a SaaS product can put that workflow in their hands.
