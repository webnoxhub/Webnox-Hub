If you’re planning a new business website, the website checklist helps you decide the pages and features you need before asking for a quote. It can also help organize a redesign brief if you already have pages, images and content you want to carry over.

If your site is ready and you’re planning a campaign, the ads checklist focuses on the path from the offer to a confirmed action. Keep the results of your first test handy so you can repeat it if you change the page, the form or the campaign later.
