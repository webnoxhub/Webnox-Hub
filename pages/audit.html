What we can review in an audit

Choose a page or a step you want to improve before you book. For a business website, it might be a service page that receives visits but few quote requests. For a store, it might be a collection, product page or checkout step. Bring the public URL and describe what a visitor should be able to do from that point.

If you have a recent report, bring a short summary of the period it covers and the metric you care about. In the conversation we can map the first checks to that goal and discuss which work would need a separate scope. Start with the site link and an example of the problem.
