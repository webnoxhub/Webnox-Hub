What you review at each step

Discovery turns your goal into pages, workflows and a list of open decisions. For a booking website, this might include the services people can book, which calendar they use and who needs to receive confirmations. Agree which parts of the site need your review and what a successful test should look like early in the project.

During design, check an important page on phone and desktop before the rest of the layouts are built. During development, test the same path with representative content. Before launch, confirm who owns the domain and where form or checkout completions are delivered.
