Paid Ads Launch Checklist

Before you spend on Google Ads or Meta Ads, check that your offer, landing page and reporting are ready.

Write down one offer and one conversion

Choose a specific offer with a clear next step. For an inquiry campaign, write down what information makes the lead useful and who follows up.

Test the page on a phone

Tap the ad destination, check that the first heading matches the offer, and complete the form or checkout. If the visitor needs to ask for a quote, make sure the confirmation page and inbox both receive your test.

Check what reporting counts

Write down which action counts as a conversion. Use a test submission to catch double counts, and check that a click on a button is tracked separately from a completed inquiry.

Set a budget and review date

Record the campaign goal, landing page URL, planned media spend and who will check the incoming leads. Keep notes on the first changes you make so you can compare results against the original setup.

Run one complete rehearsal

Open the landing page on a phone while signed out, follow the main link and send a test inquiry. Ask the person responsible for sales to confirm that it arrived with enough information to respond. If the action is a purchase, use an approved test checkout and verify the confirmation and order record.

Check which action appears in reporting and compare it to what actually happened. If submitting one form causes two conversions, resolve that before comparing campaigns. Capture the page URL, test date and who verified the result so the test can be repeated after changes.

Write down your first review questions

Decide who checks lead relevance, whether customers mention the offer they saw and what the team should do if clicks arrive without inquiries. Review the path from ad to page to follow-up before changing several things at once.

If the offer is clear and tracking is in place, the paid ads management page explains how to discuss the campaign scope. If visitors are struggling with the page itself, a landing page review is a useful place to start.

FAQ

What should I test before turning on ads?

Test the ad destination, mobile page, conversion action, confirmation and lead delivery with the same flow a real visitor will use.

Is a WhatsApp click the same as a lead?

Track it as a click. Confirm a conversation or qualified inquiry separately so your report reflects actual leads.
