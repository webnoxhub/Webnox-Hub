From the first brief to launch

Start with one action the site should make easy. A local service business may want qualified phone or WhatsApp inquiries. A company selling overseas may need a form that captures project scope, location and the best time to reply. We map the page list, navigation and contact options around that action.

The design review covers the homepage and a representative service page on phone and desktop. Before development, agree on who supplies product photos, service descriptions and any project examples. The small business website checklist gives you a brief you can fill in.

What goes into a business website

A typical scope includes responsive page layouts, a readable navigation menu and a contact path that can be tested from start to finish. If your team needs to publish regularly, we can discuss a WordPress build with editable templates. If you sell online, plan catalog and checkout through ecommerce development.

For example, a clinic may need one page per treatment category, a location page and a clear booking link. During testing, try the whole flow as a new patient. Confirm that the link opens the right provider, the phone number can be tapped and the staff know where appointment requests land.

Redesigns, launch checks and handover

For a redesign, inventory your current pages before choosing which to merge or retire. Any address that changes should have an agreed destination. We can scope page titles, internal links and crawl checks as part of SEO and website optimization.

Agree in advance on access to the domain, hosting and editing tools. At handover, review how to update a page, test a contact form and report a problem. Final cost and timing depend on content readiness, the number of layouts, and any bookings, payments or integrations in scope.
