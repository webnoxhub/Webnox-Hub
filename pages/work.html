Use an example to make your brief clearer

Pick the project type closest to the problem you need to solve. A storefront example can help you decide which product details matter before checkout. A property listings example can help you decide whether search, filters or a map belongs in the first version, and which details visitors need to see at a glance.

Describe what would have to change for the example to fit your business. A useful brief includes who will use the site, the action they need to complete and what you already have prepared. For a store, that might be a product spreadsheet. For a service business, it could be an enquiry form and sample project photographs.
