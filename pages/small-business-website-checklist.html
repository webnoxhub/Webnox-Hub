Small Business Website Checklist

Decide what your site needs before you ask for a quote. Use this checklist to plan your pages, collect your content and test the site before launch.

Choose the action your website should drive

Write down who you serve and what you want them to do after visiting. A Karachi catering business might want WhatsApp inquiries with an event date and guest count. A consultant working internationally might prefer a short inquiry form that asks for the project goal and time zone. That choice determines the main button and what information you need to collect.

List the pages and content you need

  • Home. Explain your main offer, who it serves and the next step.
  • Services. Explain what each service includes, when it is a good fit and what a customer needs to provide.
  • About and work. Add a short business introduction and relevant photos or examples you have permission to publish.
  • Contact. List the channels you actually monitor and test that messages reach the right person.

Compare quotes using the same scope

Ask each provider to price the same page list and features. Check who supplies copy and images, how many revision rounds are included and whether you can edit the site after handover. List recurring costs such as the domain, hosting and any paid tools separately. For an online store, include product entry, shipping and payment setup in the scope.

Check the site before sharing it

Try the site on a phone. Open the menu, follow the main contact button and send a test inquiry. Confirm who receives it. Check page titles, the sitemap and the link preview. For a redesign, list old URLs that are changing and make sure they redirect to the relevant new pages.

Brief to send for a quote

  • Business and audience: What you sell and which cities or countries you serve.
  • Main goal: Calls, WhatsApp messages, quote requests, bookings or sales.
  • Pages and features: Which pages you need and whether you need editing, a store or a booking tool.
  • Existing materials: Your current URL, logo, photos and any copy that is ready.
  • Budget and timeline: Your budget range, preferred launch date and any fixed deadlines.
