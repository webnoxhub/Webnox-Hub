Build the storefront around shopping decisions

List your key collections and the two or three things shoppers use to compare items. For apparel that might be fit, fabric and size. For a food business it might be portion, delivery area and lead time. Use a sample catalog to decide what belongs in navigation, filters and individual product descriptions.

Theme changes can include product templates, collection layouts and mobile adjustments. Bring a store URL or theme reference and describe the behavior you are trying to improve, such as customers missing the size guide or failing to find products that are in stock.

When to redesign or migrate a store

For an existing Shopify store, find out whether the issue is the theme, catalog organization or order flow before changing the design. Before a migration, review product and variant data, file attachments and the URLs customers already use. Decide which apps and integrations are still needed.

We scope changes against a representative product and one test order, then extend the agreed template to other products. If you’re choosing a platform from scratch, the ecommerce development page explains what to consider alongside Shopify.

Prepare the team for launch

Have someone on your team add a product, edit stock and check a real order notification during the launch rehearsal. Agree on who can edit the theme and who owns product updates, refunds and shipping settings.

If you’ll drive traffic to the store as soon as it launches, run through the paid ads launch checklist first. Test landing on the advertised collection, adding an item and completing the action that reporting will count.

FAQ

Can you improve an existing Shopify store?

Yes. Share the store link and the issues you want to fix, such as unclear navigation, hard-to-use product pages or a theme that no longer fits your brand.

Which payment options can I use?

That depends on where your business is registered, where you sell and which providers you can use. We review those details during scoping.

What should I prepare before launch?

Start with your product list, photos, prices, variants, shipping zones and store policies. These details help us scope the setup and test a purchase flow.
