Frequently Asked Questions

Can you improve an existing Shopify store?

Yes. Share the store link and the issues you want to fix, such as unclear navigation, hard-to-use product pages or a theme that no longer fits your brand.

Which payment options can I use?

That depends on where your business is registered, where you sell and which providers you can use. We review those details during scoping.

What should I prepare before launch?

Start with your product list, photos, prices, variants, shipping zones and store policies. These details help us scope the setup and test a purchase flow.
