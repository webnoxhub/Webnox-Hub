Make frequent edits easy

Decide who will update the site and what they need to edit. A small team might only change service descriptions and publish articles. A larger team may need layouts that several editors can use consistently. Build one sample page so the handover covers the actual editing experience.

Agree which elements can be changed freely and which should use a shared block, such as a quote button or contact information. This keeps common updates manageable across the site and still lets your team write pages with their own focus.

Redesign without losing useful content

Inventory articles, service pages, media and forms. Confirm which content should be migrated, rewritten or retired and where old addresses should lead after launch. A staging site gives the team a chance to review navigation and test forms before the public site changes.

If you need a store, use the ecommerce planning guide to define checkout, shipping and product data before adding WooCommerce. If the site mostly explains your services, start with a simple set of pages and expand when the content is ready.

Plan maintenance and ownership

At handover, verify that the right people can sign in, update content and recover access. Decide who is responsible for hosting, backups and plugin updates. A maintenance scope can separate routine updates from new layouts or feature requests.

For example, a professional services firm could use one service template and a separate article template. Editors can publish new topics without editing navigation or shared contact sections. Review the SEO scope if you want new pages to follow a consistent search and linking structure.

FAQ

Do I need WordPress for a small site?

WordPress is useful if you regularly edit pages or publish articles. If you need a small site with occasional updates, we can discuss a simpler custom build.

Can you redesign my site while keeping its content?

We can review what needs to move and plan the redesign around existing content. Share the current URL, which sections should stay and what your team wants to change.

Can I edit the site after handover?

Yes. During scoping we’ll agree which pages and content your team should be able to edit and what guidance you need at handover.
